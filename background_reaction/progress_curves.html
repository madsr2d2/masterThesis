Progress curves and their fits

All 27 enzyme-free BnOH cuvettes, each with every rate estimator drawn on it. Nothing here is a kinetic-model fit: the mechanism has never been fitted to these curves. These are the empirical rate measurements the analysis rests on.

What is drawn

quadratic through every point — v0 is its slope at t = 0 (headline) least-squares line over the first 20% (shaded) burst/lag form, dashed; shaded fan = v0 profile interval straight line through the whole curve ringed = suspect reading, still included in every fit

The instrument's first reading of every run is not plotted, fitted or scored. It is discarded in fit_dataset.build_curves before anything here sees a curve, unconditionally and for every run in the archive, because it is a settling artefact rather than a measurement: scored against the following 8 readings it carried median |z| 2.06 and exceeded 5σ on 15.9% of curves, where the LAST reading scored the identical way carried 1.11 and 7.5%. So the leftmost point on every panel below is the instrument's second reading, and where a footer says a ringed point is the first, it means the first plotted.

The quadratic is A = c + v0t + at². It chooses no window, uses every point, and is linear in its parameters, so v0 is always identified. Its curvature t is a/se(a): |t| > 3 means the curve measurably bends.

The burst/lag form is A = c + vsst − B(1 − e−t/τ), with v0 = vss − B/τ. The lag branch (B > 0) is opened or shut per curve, by that curve's own acceleration z-score against a 3σ gate: open on the 4 of 27 that clear it, shut elsewhere. τ is profiled rather than optimised. The shaded fan shows every initial slope allowed by a τ within the 95% cost band. Where that fan is wide the curve does not determine v0, however well the form fits: 24 of 27 curves are bounded by the 30% half-width criterion, and the rest are marked UNBOUNDED.

Fitting well is not the same as being identified. The worst case in this set is exp 3 sample 6: across statistically indistinguishable values of τ its unconstrained v0 ranges from -6.08e-06 to 7.08e-07 — a span of 39× its own fitted value and a change of sign. It is not alone: 6 of 27 curves have an unconstrained v0 interval that straddles zero. That is why v0 is profiled here and why the quadratic, not the burst form, carries the headline numbers.
