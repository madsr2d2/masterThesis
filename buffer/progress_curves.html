The buffer titrations — every progress curve

Exps 32, 34, 35, 36, 37 · 4OMe-BnOH · 40 °C · phosphate

All 20 cuvettes of the five buffer titrations — 4OMe-BnOH, 40 °C, phosphate, 82.5 mM H₂O₂, with [S] fixed inside each run — in pH order. The rust line is whichever form the curve earned, one relaxation or two, from summary_kinetics.fit_progress; nothing is excluded and every fit uses every point except the instrument's first reading, which is discarded from every run in the archive.

The blue dashed vertical is the induction landmark, read through a window of 450 seconds — in seconds, not as a share of the run, because exps 32 and 34 ran 5280 s and 1767 s and a fractional window would be two different windows. Section 6's joint order and section 4c's -0.433 ± 0.201 are read off these two quantities: the peak rate and this landmark.

Exps 32 and 34 earn different model forms, and it is visible here: every curve of exp 34 takes the two-phase form and every curve of exp 32 the one-phase, because exp 34's runs are long enough to contain the slow fall and exp 32's end before it. That is why nothing on this page compares a time constant between the two runs.
