1 · Which curves

The hand-sorted data/Mads folders needed two corrections against scope.PH_LADDERS: drop the replicate quadruplet (exps 2, 4, 5, 7) from the phosphate set and add exp 14 to it; drop exp 13 from the boric set. The pH-11 folder was already correctly excluded (build_manifest.KNOWN_EXCLUSIONS[85]).

2 · The rate's order in [HOO⁻]

Each point is one run's median across its own substrate ladder; faint rings are the two-axis block's weak runs, dropped from every fit below. Phosphate and both pyrophosphate arms climb together; the boric ladder climbs to a peak at pH 9.50 (exp 43) and then falls -- the shape section 2's single log-log slope cannot see and section 3 reads by splitting the ladder at its own peak instead.

3 · The boric ladder turns over

Median vmax rises from pH 8.46 to a peak at exp 43 (pH 9.50) and falls through pH 10.34 — below the ladder's own reading at pH 8.98. Refit below the peak, the order is +0.222 ± 0.037, positive but weaker than phosphate or pyrophosphate's. vmax_corrected moves the two highest-pH runs' medians by less than a quarter of themselves, so the O2 side reaction — heaviest in exactly this buffer and pH range — is not the cause.

4 · The clock agrees where the rate does not

Same four ladders, two responses. The rate's order in [HOO-] (filled) is where boric parts company with the other three; the induction clock's order per pH unit (open, `induction.lag_ph_ladders`) is positive on three of four and pools to +0.326 ± 0.131 with χ² = 0.95 on 3 -- the clock does not see whatever makes the boric ladder's rate turn over.

5 · The gas side reaction's own pH onset

`scope.gas_survey`, every buffer and pH band the archive holds. Zero detachments anywhere below pH 7.5; boric above pH 8.5 is the single heaviest band after pyrophosphate's own high-pH arm. The same pH region section 3 finds the catalysed RATE turning over in boric is where this unrelated side reaction is heaviest -- checked, in section 3, and not the explanation.

6 · One species, three signals, one caveat

The rate (+0.594), the induction clock (+0.347 per pH unit) and the early trough's OTHER-cuvette signal (ρ = -0.621 for 4OMe, -0.315 for BnOH against [enz]/[HOO⁻], both p < 10⁻⁴) all name [HOO⁻] rather than pH itself or total [H₂O₂]. scope.hoo_consistency is the caveat: on the two-axis block alone, moving [HOO⁻] two independent ways gives orders that part at 3.4σ — [HOO⁻] is not the whole story even where it is real.

7 · What this settles, and what it does not

Settled. The rate's order in [HOO⁻] is +0.594 ± 0.026 in phosphate and pyrophosphate. Two of those three ladders are the two-axis block (35.9% of the pooled weight), so the independent corroboration is the phosphate ladder alone — +0.596 ± 0.032 on nine runs sharing no experiment with the block, 0.83σ from its cuvette-matched +0.554 ± 0.040. The boric ladder does not share it, and the O2 side reaction is ruled out as the cause of its decline — though the order below its peak spans +0.06 to +0.48 depending on where the ladder is cut, so it is quoted as a range. The clock's order agrees across all four ladders where the rate's does not.

Not settled. Whether boric's turnover is the buffer or a genuine high-pH instability the way exp 85 showed at pH 11.84 — the archive holds no other buffer above pH 9 to separate them, which is buffer/ §5's finding applied to a new question rather than a resolution of it.
