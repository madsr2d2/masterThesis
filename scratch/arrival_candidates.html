What to look for. The grey dashed line is a quadratic through the five readings before the jump, carried forward; the blue dotted line is that same curve lifted by the measured gain. Readings that land on the lifted copy stepped — a bubble arrived. Readings that keep to the grey one, or to neither, were accelerating, and admitting them would be a mistake. The amber band is the detachment that later releases it.
